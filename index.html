Our story

Where smiles are
served daily.

Bakerz Bite is a bakery and café built around freshness, thoughtful ingredients and the joy of sharing. Our menu brings together artisan pastries, gourmet cakes, cookies, pies, desserts and handcrafted beverages.

We use real butter, cream and unbleached flour because quality belongs in every bite. Whether you're celebrating a milestone or stopping by for a coffee, we're happy you're here.

Frequently asked questions

Are your products baked fresh daily?

Yes. Our bakery team prepares a wide selection of baked goods fresh in-store every day. Availability may vary by time of day.

Can I order a celebration cake?

Yes — contact our team with your preferred size, flavour, design and collection date and we'll take care of the rest.

Where can I find ingredient information?

Tap any menu item to see its description and ingredient list. Please speak to our staff about allergies or dietary requirements before ordering.

Do you sell Bakerz Bite merchandise?

Yes — branded mugs, tote bags, glasses and serving trays are available in the Merchandise category. Contact the store to confirm current stock.

What are your opening hours?

We're open every day from 9 AM to 10 PM.
